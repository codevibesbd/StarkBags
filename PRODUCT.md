# Product: Starkbags (Stark Bag Industries Ltd.)

## Overview
Stark Bag Industries Ltd. is an export-scale luggage, backpack, and travel gear manufacturer headquartered in Uttara, Dhaka, with multi-unit factory complexes in Brahmanbaria, Bangladesh. The company produces OEM/ODM travel bags, hard-shell rolling luggage, executive backpacks, and specialized travel accessories for global retail brands and international importers.

## Primary Audience & Roles
- **Global Importers & Retail Brand Buyers:** Procurement directors, product sourcing teams, and merchandising heads in North America, Europe, Japan, and the Middle East seeking reliable, compliant, large-volume production.
- **Corporate Sourcing & OEM Partners:** Brands requiring customized luggage manufacturing, specialized material compounding (polycarbonate, ABS, PP, ballistic nylon), and private-label packaging.
- **Factory Compliance Auditors:** Quality assurance and ethical trade inspectors verifying labor standards, fire safety, environmental governance, and supply chain transparency.

## Core Capabilities & Infrastructure
- **Monthly Output:** Over 1.5 million pieces/month across dedicated lines.
- **Workforce:** 5,000+ skilled manufacturing specialists, engineers, and technicians.
- **Integrated Sister Concerns:**
  1. *Rider Leather Bags & Luggage Factory Ltd.* (Luggage & leather goods assembly)
  2. *Victor Bags Factory Ltd.* (Backpacks, daypacks, active bags)
  3. *Sensible Polymer Industries Ltd.* (Polymer injection molding, hard-case shells)
  4. *Premier Polymer Industries Ltd.* (Raw material processing and component fabrication)
  5. *Rider Packaging and Accessories Ltd.* (Carton packaging, zippers, hardware)
  6. *Sohagpur Printing Factory Ltd.* (Textile screen, heat-transfer, and digital printing)

## Product Lines
- **Hard-shell Luggage:** 20", 24", 28" spinner suitcases engineered from high-grade polycarbonate (PC), ABS, and polypropylene (PP) with TSA locks and 360-degree silent wheels.
- **Backpacks & Commuter Bags:** Water-resistant laptop bags, ergonomic school bags, outdoor tactical daypacks.
- **Travel Duffels & Holdalls:** Foldable gym bags, heavy-duty weekender bags, wheeled rolling duffels.
- **Tote & Daily Utility Bags:** Canvas, polyester, and eco-friendly shopping and shoulder bags.
